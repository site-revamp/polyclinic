# Polyclinic.ae Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Polyclinic.ae is the website of INNOVATION FAMILY POLYCLINIC L.L.C., a European multispecialty medical centre located in Jumeirah, Dubai. The clinic provides healthcare for adults and children, covering specialist consultations, dentistry, diagnostics, aesthetic medicine and preventive healthcare.

The redesigned homepage focuses on making the clinic’s wide range of services easier to navigate, presenting its key advantages more clearly and creating a more connected path from discovering healthcare services to arranging an appointment.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Healthcare Service Discovery
- Organised the presentation of medical specialties, dental services, diagnostics and other treatments into a clearer patient-focused discovery journey.
- Improved the way visitors can identify relevant departments and explore services based on their healthcare needs.
- Addresses the previous difficulty of finding the right service or specialist across the clinic’s broad offering.

### 2. Stronger Presentation of Clinic Differentiators
- Brought important clinic advantages into a more coherent presentation, including its multidisciplinary medical team, multilingual support, in-house laboratories, diagnostic facilities and insurance arrangements.
- Made these strengths easier to understand as part of the clinic’s overall value proposition.
- Addresses the fragmented presentation of information across the previous website.

### 3. More Connected Appointment and Insurance Journey
- Improved the relationship between appointment actions, contact information and insurance-related information.
- Made the next steps clearer for visitors who want to arrange a consultation or understand their insurance options.
- Addresses the disconnected journey that previously required patients to navigate separate website sections.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Content hierarchy:** Improved the organisation and presentation of healthcare information to make important services and actions easier to identify.
- **Navigation and layout:** Structured homepage sections around patient needs, supporting clearer movement between services and clinic information.
- **Conversion experience:** Gave appointment and insurance-related actions a more connected role in the patient journey.
- **Information consistency:** Presented the clinic’s services, facilities and differentiators in a more unified way.
- **Overall presentation:** Established a clearer, more organised homepage experience that communicates the clinic’s multidisciplinary healthcare offering.
